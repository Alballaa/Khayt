### Description

_2913 / 10000 characters_

```
Khayt replaces spreadsheets, chat notes and separate invoicing tools with one desktop app built specifically for 3D printing shops.

Everything runs locally. There is no account to create, no monthly fee, and no data leaves your machine unless you deliberately configure an integration.

QUOTING THAT MATCHES REALITY
Build a multi-part quote and watch the cost break down live: material, machine time, electricity, labour, overhead, failure rate and margin. FDM is costed by the gram, resin by the millilitre, and multi-material AMS/MMU jobs are handled properly. Drop in a G-code or 3MF file and Khayt reads the slicer's own print time, weight and filament type rather than asking you to guess. Volume tiers and loyalty discounts apply themselves. Export the quote as a PDF with revision history, or send an approval link the customer can accept from their phone.

A PRODUCTION QUEUE YOU CAN SEE
Drag jobs across a kanban board from Pending to Printing, Post-Processing, QC and Completed. Watch live progress rings per machine, put a job on hold with a reason, and keep a full audit log of every change order. Connect OctoPrint, Moonraker or Bambu Cloud and the board follows your printers instead of your memory.

INVOICING, INCLUDING ZATCA
Bilingual English/Arabic invoices with TLV-encoded QR codes for ZATCA Phase 1, and full Phase 2 support: ECDSA signing, CSR generation, compliance and production CSIDs, and e-invoice submission to FATOORA. Proforma and milestone invoicing, credit notes, and a GAZT VAT return export are all built in.

INVENTORY THAT DEDUCTS ITSELF
Track filament spools with automatic deduction when an order completes, FIFO or weighted-average costing, reorder alerts with draft purchase orders, a colour variants library, a spool drying log and per-supplier price history.

CLIENTS AND A CUSTOMER PORTAL
Client profiles with bilingual fields, VAT and CR numbers, credit limits and loyalty tiers. Customers get a live order-tracking page that refreshes itself, and you get referral attribution and NPS surveys.

ANALYTICS WORTH READING
Revenue trends, filament consumption, per-machine profit and loss, waste and failure costs, and a break-even view — all computed from the jobs you already logged.

MAKER TOOLS INCLUDED
A 3MF and G-code converter with 3D preview, a print-file library with per-machine slicer profiles, a colour studio for mixing and matching filament, and a print portfolio built from your own order photos.

WORKS OFFLINE, BY DESIGN
Khayt is a local desktop application. Your orders, clients and prices live in a file on your own disk. There is no cloud account, no telemetry you did not switch on, and no subscription. Optional integrations — email delivery, storefront webhooks, printer APIs, an AI quoting assistant using your own API key — are all opt-in and off until you configure them.

Available in English and Arabic, with full right-to-left layout support.
```

### What's new in this version

_935 / 1500 characters_

```
The candidate for v3.6.0 stable. There is no behaviour change over
beta.19 — it is the same code under a name that says what it is for.

The 3.6 line changes what your customers are quoted, and how every
geometry-based time estimate is worked out. That is why it has been a beta since
2026-07-31 rather than going straight to stable, and it is why there is a
release candidate at all: this installs from the same pre-release channel as the
betas and reaches nobody on stable, so the exact code proposed as v3.6.0 can be
put in front of real orders and real printer jobs before it becomes the default
for everyone.

If you price unsliced models, re-check a quote you have not yet sent. The
numbers moved in beta.9 and beta.10, for two different reasons — see
docs/BETA-RELEASE.md. Figures that came from a slicer
are unaffected; those were never estimates.

Full release notes: https://github.com/khaytapp/Khayt/releases/tag/v3.6.0-rc.1
```
