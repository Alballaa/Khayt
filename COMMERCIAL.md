Khayt's source is published under the **Functional Source License (FSL-1.1-Apache-2.0)** —
see [LICENSE](./LICENSE). That license already lets you do almost everything **for free**:

- ✅ Run Khayt to operate your 3D-printing business (any size).
- ✅ Modify it and run your own internal builds.
- ✅ Pay a consultant to set it up or support your own copy.

You only need a **commercial license** from the author if you want to do a
**Competing Use** — anything that makes Khayt available to others as a product or
service, for example:

- Selling, reselling, white-labeling, or bundling Khayt.
- Hosting Khayt (or a fork) as a paid or ad-supported service for third parties.
- Building a product or service with the same or substantially similar
  functionality on top of Khayt's code.

If that's you, or you want the software under terms other than the FSL (e.g. a
private/proprietary license, or without the 2-year Apache conversion), reach out:

**Turki AlBallaa — support@khayt.app** · https://khaytapp.com

## Hosted service

The official hosted backend, **Khayt Cloud** (cloud.khaytapp.com), is a separate
commercial offering operated by the author. The desktop app runs fully offline and
never requires it; Khayt Cloud is an optional paid convenience (sync, customer
portal, remote mobile, AI assist, web push).

## A note on the future license

Every Khayt release automatically becomes **Apache-2.0** two years after it ships
(see "Grant of Future License" in [LICENSE](./LICENSE)). After that point, that
version may be used under Apache-2.0 with no commercial restriction. The
commercial license above matters for the most recent two years of releases.
